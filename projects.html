Daralu Copper Mine — Civil and Structural Supervision

Civil & Structural Supervision | Steel Structures | Reinforced Concrete Construction | Quality Control

Worked as a Civil and Structural Supervisor at Daralu Copper Mine in Kerman Province, Iran, overseeing civil and structural construction activities across the site.

My responsibilities covered all steel structures and selected concrete buildings, including the primary crusher building. The work involved supervising construction activities, inspecting reinforcement and structural works, monitoring concrete production and placement, checking concrete slump, addressing cold-joint risks, monitoring mass-concrete temperatures, coordinating material testing, and working with contractors and site teams to resolve technical issues.

Reinforced-Concrete and Steel Building Design

Structural Analysis | Structural Design | Engineering Calculations | Technical Drawings

Performed structural analysis and design for reinforced-concrete and steel buildings of up to seven storeys. The work included structural modelling, engineering calculations, preparation of structural drawings, and application of relevant Iranian building regulations and seismic design requirements.

Software used included ETABS, SAFE, and SAP2000.
