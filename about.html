About Me

I am a Civil Engineer with experience in structural design, freelance engineering, civil and structural supervision, and construction quality control across building and industrial projects.

Having worked in both design and construction environments, I understand the importance of connecting engineering calculations and drawings with the practical realities of construction. My experience includes reinforced-concrete and steel structures, structural analysis, engineering calculations, and supervising construction activities on large-scale industrial projects.

I value accurate calculations, practical solutions, clear technical documentation, and attention to construction quality. I believe that effective engineering requires both a sound understanding of technical principles and the ability to apply them under real project conditions.

I am also interested in using AI tools to investigate engineering questions, interpret technical references, and improve problem-solving workflows. I see AI as a tool to support engineering work, while engineering judgment, independent verification, and compliance with applicable codes and standards remain essential.
