Professional Journey

As a young man, I was interested in construction and problem-solving, which led me to pursue a degree in Civil Engineering at Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman. I later continued my education with a master's degree in Civil Engineering, specializing in Hydraulic Structures. After completing my studies, I began my professional career at ATIE-NEGAR-KALUT-KAVIR, where I gained my first professional experience in structural design.

Structural Design and Freelance Work

At ATIE-NEGAR-KALUT-KAVIR, I worked on reinforced-concrete and steel building projects, carrying out structural modelling, engineering calculations, and structural drawing preparation using ETABS, SAFE, and SAP2000.

After leaving the company, I continued working as a freelance structural engineer, undertaking building design projects and preparing structural calculations and drawings. This allowed me to continue developing my experience in building design alongside other professional responsibilities.

My First Large-Scale Industrial Project

My first experience with large-scale industrial construction came at Daralu Copper Mine in Kerman Province. I joined Kian Madan Pars as a Civil and Structural Supervisor, taking responsibility for supervising all steel structures and selected concrete buildings across the site, including the primary crusher.

My work involved supervising construction activities, monitoring concrete production and placement, checking concrete slump, addressing cold-joint risks, monitoring mass-concrete temperatures, inspecting reinforcement, coordinating material testing, and working with contractors and site teams to resolve technical issues. This experience gave me practical insight into the challenges of delivering large industrial structures while maintaining construction quality and meeting technical requirements.

Engineering and AI-Assisted Problem-Solving

Alongside my experience in structural design and construction supervision, I have developed an interest in using AI tools to solve practical engineering problems. I use these tools to investigate technical questions, interpret engineering references, and explore more efficient ways to approach engineering tasks.

My aim is to combine practical engineering experience with technology while maintaining sound engineering judgment and verifying technical conclusions against applicable codes and standards.
